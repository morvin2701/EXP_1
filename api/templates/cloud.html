How it works

🔗

Copy the link

Open any public reel or post in Instagram and tap Share → Copy Link.

📥

Paste & download

Paste it above and hit Download. Video → MP4, photos → JPG, carousels → ZIP.

💻

Need private / big files?

Run the free local app on your own computer for private accounts and full-size videos.
