How it works

In Instagram, open any reel or post and tap Share → Copy Link.

📥

Connect Instagram

Needed for private accounts, and if Instagram blocks anonymous downloads.

The most reliable method. Be logged in to instagram.com in Chrome, Safari or Firefox, then click above. If macOS asks for Keychain access, enter your Mac password and press “Always Allow”. Nothing leaves your computer.

  1. Open instagram.com (logged in) and press F12
  2. Go to Application → Cookies → instagram.com
  3. Copy the value of sessionid and paste it above

⚠️ Instagram often rejects password sign-ins from tools even with the correct password, or asks for a security checkpoint. If that happens, use the Browser tab instead.
